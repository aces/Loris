# New Profile

This is where you can register a new candidate for your study site. 

Populate the fields and click **Create**. 

Every new candidate will be assigned a six-digit DCCID (Data Coordinating Center ID). The candidate can now be identified by their DCCID, along with their pre-assigned unique PSCID (Project Study Center ID).

Additional candidate identifier fields may appear depending on the selected project and your permissions. Enter values for the manual identifiers and select the identifiers you wish to generate automatically. Required identifiers must be provided or generated during registration. Some identifier types allow multiple values.
